    Little tips for big adventures

    🌸

    When to go

    Late March to April (cherry blossoms) and October to November (autumn leaves) are the sweet spots. Summer is hot and humid.

    🚇

    Getting around

    Get an IC card (Suica or PASMO) for subways, trains and even vending machines. Avoid the rush hours around 8–9 am and 6–7 pm.

    🚅

    Bullet trains

    The Shinkansen leaves from Tokyo Station. Reserve a seat, and grab an ekiben bento box for the ride.

    💴

    Paying

    Cards and IC cards work in most places, but small shops and old-school ramen counters are often cash only. Keep some yen. Tipping is not expected.
